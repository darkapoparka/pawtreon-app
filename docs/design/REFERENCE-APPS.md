# Reference apps for Pawtreon

Research date: 2026-09-09. These are selected references, not an objective ranking, a promise of identical regional availability, or owner approval to replace Pawtreon's design.

## Recommendation

Keep the earlier two-phone Pawtreon concept as the art-direction reference. Use Airbnb's 2026 discovery presentation as the main external visual benchmark, GoFundMe for fundraiser journeys, and ShareTheMeal for focused giving. Patreon is a secondary reference for following organizers and viewing updates; its broader content/membership product is not the v1 scope. Do not combine four different visual systems or restart the installed application.

## Airbnb â€” primary external visual benchmark

The official 20 May 2026 announcement shows a search-first homepage, a category row and rounded photographic destination cards. It also describes shared itinerary maps. The release announced a staged rollout; this review does not establish every feature is available to every account today.

Study: search hierarchy, spacing around photography, card information hierarchy, optional map discovery and transitions from list to detail. Adapt those patterns to named organizers and funding information. Do not copy travel categories, nightly prices, ratings, booking calendars, trip tools, promotional credits or the complete navigation.

Official release with images: https://news.airbnb.com/airbnb-2026-summer-release/
Official discovery image: https://news.airbnb.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2026/05/en-US-NewsroomHeader.png?w=2048

## GoFundMe â€” closest fundraising product reference

GoFundMe's official app page describes starting/managing fundraisers, discovering by category/location, donating, posting updates and thanking donors. That is the closest functional match among this shortlist.

Study: fundraiser setup, beneficiary/organizer context, progress, giving, updates and fundraiser management. Keep Pawtreon's own visual identity. Do not copy GoFundMe branding, guarantees, tax language, donor-advised funds or assume their payment/country model transfers to Pawtreon.

Official app overview: https://www.gofundme.com/c/the-gofundme-app
Developer app listing: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gofundme-fundraise-and-give/id734130700
Official update flow: https://support.gofundme.com/hc/en-us/articles/12553895153563-How-to-post-a-GoFundMe-update

## ShareTheMeal â€” focused giving and clear outcomes

The UN developer listing presents donations to specific causes, visibility into where support goes and community challenges. WFP USA publishes side-by-side campaign and amount-selection screenshots.

Study: one clear giving task at a time, visible cause, amount selection, progress and dated outcome updates. Use Pawtreon's own neutral/teal design, not a copied blue/yellow scheme. Do not invent a fixed amount-to-meals or amount-to-treatment conversion. Recurring giving and challenges are not added to v1 by this reference.

Developer app listing: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sharethemeal-charity-donate/id977130010
Official walkthrough with screenshots: https://wfpusa.org/news/sharethemeal-app-fights-hunger-in-one-tap/
Official giving/account FAQ: https://sharethemeal.org/en-us/faq

## Patreon â€” secondary, later-use reference

The current help guide describes supported-creator content, profiles, notifications and membership management. Study the relationship between an organizer profile and its updates. Do not bring in paid content, membership tiers, chats or a social discovery feed just because Patreon has them.

Official current guide: https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/19501240539405-Navigate-the-Patreon-app-as-a-member

## Evidence boundaries

Official published materials and the Airbnb/ShareTheMeal images were inspected. Complete authenticated app journeys were not captured or tested. The Mobbin connector returned a paid-plan restriction; no Mobbin screens or flows were downloaded. Sources may depict selected releases or promotional data, not an exhaustive live state. References are not production assets, licenses to another app's code/branding, or evidence of Pawtreon functionality.

Next review should select specific reference screens for a flow, rather than asking a generator to vaguely imitate an entire app. Keep third-party references separate from Pawtreon concepts and real rescue evidence.
